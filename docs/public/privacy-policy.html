Privacy Policy for AdPayOn

Effective Date: October 23, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

Transparency Commitment: AdPayOn ("we", "our", or "us") provides an educational learning, task discovery, and reward platform. We respect your privacy, enforce data minimization, and adhere strictly to Google Play Data Safety, applicable financial regulations, and digital personal data protection standards.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum personal information strictly necessary to deliver platform features, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements:

  • Account Credentials: Mobile phone number (+91 format) used for one-time password (OTP) verification and authentication.
  • Task Telemetry & Activity Data: Time spent reading educational articles, video playback session completion events, comprehension quiz answers, and user feedback submissions.
  • Device & Network Data: IP address, device operating system version, and client user-agent strictly for account security, rate-limiting, and fraud prevention. We do NOT harvest device contacts, media galleries, location GPS, or scan installed application packages.
  • KYC & Banking Information (Withdrawal Earners Only): When you request a payout of earned rewards, we collect your Full Name, Government Identity Document (PAN / Aadhaar) for KYC identity validation, and your verified Bank Account Number / IFSC code or UPI ID to execute the bank payout.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authentication: Cryptographic verification of phone numbers via SMS OTP.
  • Reward Verification: Evaluating genuine engagement with tasks before crediting promotional reward points to your immutable ledger balance.
  • Fraud Prevention & Integrity: Detecting multi-accounting, automated bots, playback emulation, and suspicious withdrawal patterns.
  • Financial Compliance: Fulfilling mandatory Know-Your-Customer (KYC) requirements, tax reporting, and anti-money laundering (PMLA) obligations.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers or marketing firms. Data is disclosed only to trusted infrastructure providers strictly under confidential processing agreements:

  • Cloud Hosting & Database: Render Inc. (API hosting) and MongoDB Atlas (encrypted database storage).
  • SMS Gateways: Authorized telecom aggregators for transmitting one-time login OTPs.
  • Banking & Payout Partners: RBI-licensed banking partners and payment aggregators (e.g., Razorpay / Cashfree) strictly to disburse approved wallet withdrawals directly to your account.

4. Data Retention & Mandatory Account Deletion

In full compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated personal data instantly from within the AdPayOn mobile application via Profile Settings → Delete Account & Personal Data.
  • Public Web Deletion: If you have uninstalled the app or lost access, you can submit a deletion request via our public portal at https://adpayon.com/privacy/delete-account.
  • Erasure Process: Upon deletion, your login tokens are immediately revoked, your user status is flagged DELETED, and your personal identifiers (phone number, name, device fingerprints) are scrubbed and anonymized.
  • Statutory Retention: In compliance with Indian financial compliance regulations (PMLA 2002), completed financial withdrawal ledger transactions are retained in a secure, read-only audit archive for the statutory retention period (up to 5 years).

5. Security & Encryption

All data transmitted between the AdPayOn mobile app, Admin Dashboard, and backend servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Sensitive database fields and authentication tokens are cryptographically salted and hashed.
