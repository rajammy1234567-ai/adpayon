Request Account & Data Deletion

Important Warning: Requesting account deletion will immediately terminate your AdPayOn session, forfeit any unredeemed points or pending task submissions, and purge your personal profile data.

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy and applicable data privacy regulations, AdPayOn allows all users to request complete deletion of their account and associated data without requiring the app to be installed.

Types of Data Deleted:

  • User Account Profile (Registered phone number, name, authentication credentials)
  • Active and historical session tokens (immediate revocation)
  • Task interaction history, quiz attempts, and user feedback text
  • Unprocessed KYC document files and personal banking credentials

Data Retained for Legal Compliance:

  • Financial ledger transaction history for completed monetary withdrawals is retained for up to 5 years strictly to fulfill mandatory Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA 2002) and tax compliance regulations. Retained records are kept in encrypted, access-restricted cold storage and never used for marketing.
